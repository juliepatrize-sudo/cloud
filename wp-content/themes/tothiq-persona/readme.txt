A bilingual (English + Arabic) personal-brand block theme for consultants, founders, coaches, speakers, authors and executives.

== Description ==

Enter your details once in Appearance → Customize → Your details — name, role, short bio, city, phone, WhatsApp, booking link and social profiles — and the header, hero, buttons and footer all follow.

Every button books a call or opens WhatsApp. There is no contact form to configure: "Book a call" goes to your Calendly, Cal.com or other booking page, or to WhatsApp when you have none.

The portrait is an ordinary image block: click it and choose Replace to use your own. A cut-out on a transparent background looks best.

Three landing pages sit next to the home page, each with its own colours and layout: a Course page (a price card that stays in view, seats left, curriculum by week, Enrol on WhatsApp), a Webinar page (a live countdown, Save my seat on WhatsApp, Add to calendar, speakers and agenda) and a Book page (a cover drawn from your title, buy buttons, formats, chapters and reviews). Two product pages sell one product each on WhatsApp: a Perfume page (sizes, notes, story, gift options) and a Lipstick page (shades by family, the chosen shade on four skin tones, every shade side by side, reviews by skin tone). Add a page and choose the page type under Template. The webinar's start time follows your site's time zone under Settings → General.

For English and Arabic on one site, install the free Polylang plugin and add Arabic as a second language. Every section, the menu and the footer follow the language of the page.
